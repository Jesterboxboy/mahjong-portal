What data we process

Visiting the website
The server logs IP address, time, requested page and browser identifier to run and secure the website (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The logs are rotated automatically and overwritten continuously.
Cookies
We only set technically necessary cookies (login, language setting, form protection). There are no analytics, tracking or advertising cookies.
User account
Email address, name and password (never stored in plain text) to provide the account (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), until the account is deleted.
Tournament registration
Name, country, city, email address and optionally phone number and EMA ID to run the tournament (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The public participant list shows first name, first letter of the last name and country; logged-in users with a player profile see the full name, and all logged-in users see the payment status for tournaments with an entry fee. Details are in the terms and conditions linked on each tournament page.
Results, rankings and player profiles
Name, country, city, tournament results and ratings, for online tournaments also player names and public statistics from Tenhou and Mahjong Soul. We publish them as part of the tournament and ranking history (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
Tournament photos
We publish photos from tournaments without names (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
Association members
Name and membership fees for managing the association (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR), as long as statutory retention obligations apply.

Recipients

  • Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany) hosts the website (processor).
  • gdsolutions e.U. operates the Pantheon scoring system and email delivery on our behalf (processor).
  • For EMA-rated tournaments we send name, country, EMA ID and result to the European Mahjong Association, which publishes them on mahjong-europe.org.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. If you object to publication, we anonymise your entries or take your photos down. Write to vorstand@riichimahjong.at.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority: www.dsb.gv.at.
